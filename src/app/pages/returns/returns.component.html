Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

We want you to be happy with every Khurana Kitchenware purchase. This policy explains when a return, replacement or refund applies, and how to request one.

1. Eligibility for Returns

We accept returns or replacements only for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect (i.e. not what you ordered). Because of the nature of kitchenware and hygiene considerations, we're unable to accept returns simply due to change of mind once an item has been delivered.

2. Reporting a Problem

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order details and clear photos or a short video showing the damage, defect or discrepancy. Requests raised after this window may not be eligible for a return or refund, as it becomes difficult to confirm the issue occurred prior to delivery.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Items that have been used, washed, or altered from their original condition cannot be returned. Products sold as part of a discounted bundle or clearance sale are final sale unless received damaged or defective.

4. How to Start a Return

Reach out via our Contact Us page with your order number, the product(s) affected, and supporting photos/video. Our team will review your request and respond with next steps, typically within 2–3 business days.

5. Replacements

Where a replacement is available for the same product, we'll prioritise sending a replacement at no additional cost once the issue is verified. If a replacement isn't available, we'll offer a refund instead.

6. Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method via our payment gateway. Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to reflect in your account, depending on your bank or card issuer's processing times. You'll receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated on our end.

7. Return Shipping

For approved returns due to our error (damaged, defective, or incorrect items), we'll arrange or reimburse reasonable return shipping. We may ask you to retain the original packaging until the return is resolved.

8. Bulk & Corporate Orders

Bulk and corporate gifting orders are subject to the terms agreed at the time of order confirmation, which may include separate return/replacement terms discussed directly with our team.

9. Contact Us

Questions about a specific order? Contact our support team — we're happy to help sort things out.
