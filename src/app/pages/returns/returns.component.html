1. General Policy

2. Eligible Cases for Replacement

A replacement request shall be considered only if:

  • The product received is damaged during transit;
  • The product received is defective due to a manufacturing defect;
  • The Customer receives an incorrect product different from the order placed.

No replacement shall be provided for normal wear and tear, improper handling, misuse, negligence, accidental damage, or dissatisfaction arising from personal preferences.

3. Cases Where Returns, Refunds or Replacements Will Not Be Accepted

The Company shall not accept any request for return, replacement, or refund in the following circumstances:

  • Change of mind after purchase;
  • Customer ordered the wrong product, size, colour, model, or quantity;
  • Product has been used, installed, assembled, altered, modified, repaired, or tampered with;
  • Product is damaged due to improper use, negligence, mishandling, or external causes;
  • Minor variations in colour, finish, dimensions, weight, texture, or packaging which do not affect the functionality of the product;
  • Damage caused after successful delivery;
  • Products purchased under clearance sale, promotional offers, special discounts, or "No Return" offers unless defective upon delivery;
  • Any complaint made after the prescribed reporting period of two (2) days including the date of sale.

4. Reporting a Damaged or Incorrect Product

The Customer must inspect the package immediately upon delivery. Any claim regarding a damaged product, manufacturing defect, or incorrect product must be reported within 48 hours from the time of delivery by emailing support@khuranakitchenware.com (or such other email address notified by the Company).

The complaint must include:

  • Order Number;
  • Invoice;
  • Description of the issue;
  • Clear photographs of the outer packaging, shipping label, inner packaging, product from multiple angles, and the damaged portion (if any); and
  • Unboxing video, wherever available.

Failure to provide the above information may result in rejection of the claim.

5. Mandatory Unboxing Video

For products claimed to be damaged, broken, missing parts, or incorrectly supplied, the Company may require an uninterrupted unboxing video starting from the unopened sealed package. In the absence of sufficient evidence, including the unboxing video wherever demanded, the Company reserves the right to reject the claim.

6. Verification Process

Every complaint shall undergo verification by the Company's quality control team. The Company may seek additional photographs or videos, arrange inspection by its logistics partner, or request return of the product for technical examination. The decision of the Company after such verification shall be final, subject to applicable law.

7. Replacement Process

If the claim is approved, the Company may arrange pickup of the defective product or request the Customer to courier the product to the designated address. Replacement shall be processed only after inspection of the returned product (where applicable) and confirmation that the reported defect exists.

The replacement shall be subject to product availability. If the same product is unavailable, the Company may, at its sole discretion, provide a substantially similar product, or issue a refund where replacement is not reasonably possible.

8. Refund Policy

Refunds shall be issued only in the following circumstances:

  • the Company is unable to replace the defective product;
  • the ordered product cannot be supplied due to non-availability after payment.

Refunds shall not be granted merely because the Customer no longer requires the product, the Customer changes his/her mind, delivery is refused without valid reason, or the Customer failed to read the product description before placing the order.

Approved refunds shall generally be processed within 7–15 working days through the original mode of payment. Banking timelines may vary. Shipping charges, convenience fees, payment gateway charges, gift wrapping charges, or other service charges are non-refundable unless otherwise determined by the Company.

Customers may request cancellation only before dispatch. Once dispatched, cancellation requests may not be accepted.

Before accepting delivery, Customers should carefully inspect the package. If the package appears tampered, opened, torn, leaking, or severely damaged, the Customer should refuse delivery and immediately email the Company with photographs. Acceptance of visibly damaged packaging without appropriate remarks to the delivery personnel may adversely affect the claim.
